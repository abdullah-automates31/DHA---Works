## Worker-app improvement document (3 Nov) and Shariq's confirmed answers (3 Nov)

Built in the worker app and the shared database (migrations 0024 to 0026): the materials toggle with an itemised list and an automatic estimated material cost; "Other" removed from the quote form; "Not chosen" is now "Rejected"; the customer chooses the schedule and the worker cannot; the status timeline turns "Completed" green with a tick; rejected and cancelled jobs are hidden from workers in the database. Shariq's answers:

1. **ASAP scheduling: decided.** The customer app must require a date and time for every job, including ASAP. The customer's schedule is the single source of truth. **Customer app (Shariq), not built here.** The database does not enforce it yet (changing `post_job` would break the customer app until it asks for a date); until the customer app does, an ASAP job is scheduled with no time and the worker is told to call the customer.
2. **Other: decided.** Only the quote's "Other (Rs)" line is removed. Keep the report reason "Something else", the `other` report kind and the columns `other_amount` and `other_note`.
3. **Cancellation notification: built.** It reads "This job has been cancelled. Do not travel to the customer's address." and opens nothing. A cancelled job stays invisible and inaccessible to workers (also to the worker who had accepted it).
4. **Lost jobs: confirmed.** A worker who loses a job (rejected, or the customer chose someone else while the job is closed) sees a bare "Rejected" card and cannot open or quote on it. A replaced or rejected worker stays excluded if the job reopens (Shariq confirmed 3 Nov: "No he cannot quote again. The rejected job will appear as rejected only. No status will be shown." Already built: the quote becomes `rejected`, the job is hidden and shows as one bare "Rejected" card). Also confirmed by Shariq (4 Nov): a worker who lost because the job went to another worker cannot quote again either. Both cases stay excluded, which is how the database already works (no change needed).
5. **Material bill: decided, built (0026).** A quote carries the itemised materials list and the estimated material cost, no bill. The actual bill is uploaded when the job is completed, and only when the quote has materials above 0 (older quotes too). `quotes.material_bill_path` and `resident_quotes.material_bill_path` stay in the database, unused, so nothing reading them breaks.
6. **Customer app (Shariq), not built here:** show "Rejected" instead of "Not Chosen"; stop showing the quote's visit window (new quotes have none: `resident_quotes.visit_start` and `visit_end` are null); keep showing the customer's own job schedule.
7. **Schedule restrictions: built.** Workers cannot change the schedule in the app or the API (no write access to `jobs`; `submit_quote` ignores any visit time). The Scheduled screen tells a late worker to call the customer.
